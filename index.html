      How to play

      • Lanes. Steer the ship across three lanes to catch coloured blocks. They land on the beat.

      • The 3×7 grid. Each block you catch drops into the grid column of its lane.

      • Matches. Three or more touching blocks make a match. A short timer starts; when it runs out the match cashes in for 35 × blocks² points.

      • Overfill. A column that fills up cashes the grid in at once. The column’s rails glow orange-red when it is full.

      • Greys. Grey blocks take the top block off their column. In Ninja, spikes wipe the whole grid.

      • Power blocks. They multiply a live match, or copy your grid when none is pending.
